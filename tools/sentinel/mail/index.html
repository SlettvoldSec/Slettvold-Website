What the grade means

The grade weighs DMARC most, because it is the check that tells other mail servers to refuse fake mail. A domain without DMARC can't get better than D, and one that only monitors (p=none) can't get better than C. DKIM keys are found by guessing common selector names; if yours is not found, that does not mean it is missing.
