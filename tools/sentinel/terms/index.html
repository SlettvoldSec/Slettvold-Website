Short and plain. If anything is unclear, write to tommy@slettvold.org.

Who provides the service

Sentinel (formerly Mail Shield) is made by Slettvold Security (org.nr. 935886457), Norway. Subscriptions are sold through Stripe Managed Payments: Stripe is the seller of record and handles payment, receipts, sales tax and refunds under its own terms.

What you get

For each verified domain on your plan, Sentinel checks:

  • Email security once a day: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MTA-STS, TLS-RPT, BIMI and MX.
  • DNS every hour: the A, AAAA, MX, NS, CAA and TXT records.
  • Security headers every hour: HSTS, Content-Security-Policy, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy and frame protection on the front page.
  • Uptime every 5 minutes: whether the front page answers over HTTPS, and how fast.
  • Certificate and domain expiry once a day, from public Certificate Transparency logs and registration data (RDAP).

You get an email when something gets worse: the email grade drops or a record disappears, a DNS record changes, a security header disappears, the site does not answer twice in a row (and when it is back), or the certificate or domain registration is about to expire. The plans cover 1, 5 or 20 domains. Earlier Mail Shield plans keep their price and number of domains, and now include all of the checks above.

All checks are passive: they only read what your domain shows to everyone. Sentinel does not scan ports, does not try to log in, does not send test emails and never changes your DNS.

Your domains

You may only add domains you own or are allowed to manage. Each domain must be verified with a TXT record before it is monitored.

Price, renewal and cancelling

The subscription renews every month at the price shown when you bought it. You can cancel at any time, and monitoring then runs until the end of the period you have paid for. To cancel, use the link in your Stripe receipt or write to me. Your statutory consumer rights are not affected.

Limits

I run the service with care, but lookups can fail, public services such as Certificate Transparency logs and RDAP can be slow or incomplete, and no monitor finds every problem, so an alert may arrive late or not at all. Sentinel is guidance, not a guarantee. As far as the law allows, my liability is limited to what you paid for the service in the last 12 months.

Your data

What is stored and for how long is described in the privacy policy. In short: your email address, your domains and their check results. Uptime statistics and alerts are kept for 90 days, and everything is deleted 30 days after the subscription ends.

Changes and law

If these terms change in a way that matters to you, I tell you by email at least 30 days before. Norwegian law applies.
