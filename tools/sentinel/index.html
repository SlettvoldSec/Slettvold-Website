What Sentinel watches

Email security

SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MTA-STS and more, graded A–F, once a day. Alert when the grade drops or a record disappears.

DNS changes

A, AAAA, MX, NS, CAA and TXT, every hour. An unexpected change to your mail or name servers is often the first sign of a hijacked account.

Uptime

Your front page over HTTPS every 5 minutes. Alert after two failures in a row, and again when it is back.

Certificate

Expiry date and issuer from public Certificate Transparency logs. Alerts 30, 14 and 3 days before it runs out.

Security headers

HSTS, Content-Security-Policy, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy and frame protection. Alert when one disappears.

Domain expiry

The registration date of the domain itself, from RDAP. Alerts 30 and 7 days before.

Safe by design

Sentinel only reads what your domain already shows to everyone: public DNS, the certificate in public logs, the response of your front page and the public registration data. It never scans ports, tries to log in or sends test emails. Every domain has to be verified with a TXT record before the first check, so nobody can point Sentinel at a domain that is not theirs.

Your data stays in the EU, there is no tracking, and the details are in the privacy policy.
