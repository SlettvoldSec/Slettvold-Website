鬼道場From your first hiragana to JLPT N1

Oni Dojo is a complete Japanese course taught by Kurenai 紅, a slightly mischievous oni sensei. Every lesson walks you through new words, grammar, a short dialogue and a checkpoint, and every sentence is voiced.

本
142 guided lessons

N5 to N1, with grammar explained in plain English and a checkpoint at the end of every lesson.

声
Natural voices

Over 16,000 recorded clips, so you hear real pitch and rhythm in every word and sentence.

札
Flashcards that stick

Spaced repetition for words, kanji and grammar, plus speaking cards that make you produce the word.

会
Talk with Kurenai

Role-plays you answer by typing or out loud, with several right answers and branching endings.

読
Stories at your level

44 graded stories and 38 culture articles, with a meter that shows how many words you already know.

静
No ads, no tracking

Works offline. No ads, no analytics. An account is optional.

教え方How Kurenai teaches

Built on what language-learning research keeps finding works, not on streak tricks. No hearts, no energy, no waiting to play.

書
You write the answer

Many gaps are typed, not tapped: kana, kanji or romaji all count. Producing a word fixes it better than picking it.

考
A hint before the answer

Get it wrong and Kurenai first gives you a hint and one more try. The full explanation comes after.

聴
Hear it, write it

Every dialogue ends with a short dictation, so listening and spelling grow together.

影
Shadowing in five steps

Listen, read along, whisper along, shadow without the text, then record yourself and compare your melody.

漢
Furigana that fades

The reading above a kanji disappears once you know the word well, so your eyes learn to read it.

記
Reviews at the right time

Flashcards use FSRS, a memory model that learns how fast you forget each card and brings it back just in time.

質問Questions

Is Oni Dojo free?

Yes. All hiragana and katakana and the whole JLPT N5 course are free, with their stories, flashcards and games, and there are no ads. Premium opens N4 to N1.

How far does it go?

From your first hiragana to JLPT N1: 142 guided lessons (25 at N5, 27 at N4 and 30 each at N3, N2 and N1), graded stories, culture articles and mock exams for every level.

Is it good for the JLPT?

Yes. Each level follows the JLPT, and the JLPT path has mock exams with the real section minimums, a ten-minute exam sprint and a countdown to the next test in July or December.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Lessons, voices, stories and flashcards are all on your phone. You only need the internet to sign in or buy Premium.

Do I need an account?

No. An account is optional and free; it lets Premium follow you to a new phone. Oni Dojo has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Which languages does the app speak?

Menus and messages are in English or Norwegian (bokmål). Lessons and explanations are in English for now.

Is there a PC version?

A Windows version is in testing. If you want to hear when it is ready, write to tommy@slettvold.org.

How is it different from Duolingo?

Oni Dojo is a full course: every grammar point is explained, every sentence is voiced, and it goes all the way to N1. There are no hearts or energy to run out of, and you can study as long as you like.
