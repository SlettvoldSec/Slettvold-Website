鬼道場From your first hiragana to JLPT N1

Oni Dojo is a complete Japanese course taught by Kurenai 紅, a slightly mischievous oni sensei. Every lesson walks you through new words, grammar, a short dialogue and a checkpoint, and every sentence is voiced.

本
140 guided lessons

N5 to N1, with grammar explained in plain English and a checkpoint at the end of every lesson.

声
Natural voices

Over 15,000 recorded clips, so you hear real pitch and rhythm in every word and sentence.

札
Flashcards that stick

Spaced repetition for words, kanji and grammar, plus speaking cards that make you produce the word.

会
Talk with Kurenai

Role-plays you answer by typing or out loud, with several right answers and branching endings.

読
Stories at your level

44 graded stories and 38 culture articles, with a meter that shows how many words you already know.

静
No ads, no tracking

Works offline. No ads, no analytics. An account is optional.

プレミアムFree to start

All kana and the whole N5 course are free, with their stories, flashcards and games. Oni Dojo Premium opens N4 to N1 and every new lesson. With a free account, Premium works on all your devices.

Free
Always free

Hiragana, katakana and all of N5. No ads, no time limit.

Premium
Yearly or monthly

Everything up to N1, new lessons as they come. Yearly starts with 7 days free. Cancel any time in Google Play.

Lifetime
Pay once

Everything, forever, without a subscription. Lifetime members also get the Founder mark and Kin 金, a gold theme, plus every perk added later.

Where your support goes. Oni Dojo is made by one person. Most of what comes in from Premium and Lifetime goes straight back into the app: more lessons and stories, more voices, and bigger features such as spoken conversations with Kurenai.
