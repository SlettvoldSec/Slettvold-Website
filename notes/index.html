Oni Dojo is heading to Google Play

Oni Dojo started as the Japanese course I wanted for myself. It now runs on Windows and Android, with Kurenai as a glowing oni mask, over 15,000 voice recordings, and conversations you can answer out loud.

On Android, kana and the whole N5 course are free. Premium unlocks everything up to N1. There are no ads and no tracking, because a learning app shouldn't need them. The closed test starts soon; write to me if you'd like to join.
