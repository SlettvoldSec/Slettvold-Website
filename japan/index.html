My Japanese name日本の名前

平原ひらはら 智明ともあき
平原

Hirahara: my surname, translated

平 means flat and 原 a plain or meadow. That's exactly what Slettvold means in Norwegian: slett (flat) + voll (meadow). It's also a real Japanese family name.

智明

Tomoaki: close to Tommy

智 means wisdom and 明 bright or clear. Tomoaki is a common Japanese first name that sounds close to Tommy.

On official papers in Japan my name is written as in my passport, in katakana: トミー・アンドレ・スレットボルド.
